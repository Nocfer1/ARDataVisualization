# First course session
Our first week was spent on brainstorming and finding suitable project ideas for both the upcoming AR solution and the latter VR project. The requirement of having to solve a real world problem helped us significantly, as it allowed us to narrow our focus down to solutions that leverage the advantages of their respective platforms. For example, we iterated through multiple ideas for the AR project  that combined many of the features offered by modern smartphones. First we started with a 'path finding' solution, which would help users get from point A to point B by displaying a path to follow in their environment - reminiscent of a video game 'yellow line' showing the optimal way to get to an objective. While this idea made great use of the sensors available in a smartphone, like GPS positioning and IMU readings, it wouldn't make great use of the AR capabilities, as the same could easily be done on a map rather than in a camera overlay. However, the idea for using GPS positioning stayed with us and directed our future idea brainstorming.

We followed it with an idea for visualizing the structures hidden behind walls, such as cables or pipes, that would be useful to know in home renovation settings.
